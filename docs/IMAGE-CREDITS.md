## Licensing Guidelines
1. **Source Integrity**: Real photographs only, supplied directly by client/authorized photographer.
2. **AI Restriction**: Zero AI-generated portraits or synthetic imagery.
3. **No False Endorsement**: No portrait of any person may be placed directly adjacent to a testimonial, rating, or quote.
4. **Current Repository Status**: All photo slots currently resolve to the procedural high-contrast Navy gradient fallback (`#07142B` to `#0D2147`) pending final client assets.
